Using the output

Use quantities for this shipment if the order ships in parts. Packing slips list contents and leave payment and price information on the invoice.

When to use the packing slip generator

A packing slip lists what is inside a parcel, so the person receiving it can check the contents. It normally leaves prices out, which makes it suitable for gifts and for orders where payment is shown on a separate invoice.

If an order ships in more than one parcel, make a slip for each parcel with only the quantities in that box.

Worked example

  1. Enter your business, the order reference ORDER-1024 and the dispatch date.
  2. Add the items: 1 × Lavender candle 200g (SKU MW-CAN-LAVENDER-200G-001) and 1 × Travel tin.
  3. Add a short note, such as how to get in touch about a missing item.
  4. Generate the A4 PDF, then print it and place it in the parcel.

Common questions

Should a packing slip show prices?

Usually not. Keep payment details on the invoice, so a slip can go in gift orders and partial shipments without confusion.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP logo; it is used in the PDF generated in your browser.

Is a packing slip the same as a shipping label?

No. The shipping label goes on the outside of the parcel for the carrier; the packing slip goes inside for the recipient.
