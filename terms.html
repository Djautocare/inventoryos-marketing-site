14. Till payments, staff and offline records

InventoryOS currently records card payments that your staff confirm after using your separate terminal. It does not trigger the terminal, verify settlement or move the sale proceeds. Your payment provider’s contract and fees are separate. You remain responsible for checking the terminal result, cash received, change, refunds, permissions, float and closing balance.

Staff should use their own number or QR and private PIN. Only authorised managers should approve restricted actions. You control staff assignments and shop access. Restricted staff tills require internet access. Prepared back-office cash sales may be queued on the original device and synced after reconnecting; they are not a guarantee of uninterrupted offline trading. Do not clear browser data or delete a shop while sales are pending.

Production tools are included in Pro. Existing Business subscriptions retain their existing manufacturing entitlement. Manufacturing is off by default. You remain responsible for recipes, measurement units, safe production, actual finished quantities, batches, expiry dates, recalls and regulatory records. Confirm work is actually complete before recording production. Saved timers do not make products, certify safety or confirm cleaning.

Barcode lookups, carrier detection, label cropping, supplier price checks, reorder quantities and suggested prices can be incomplete or incorrect. Review the source and the proposed action before use. A barcode match does not set your selling price or prove product safety. Supplier pages can change or be unavailable; do not provide supplier passwords. These features do not guarantee stock availability, profit or regulatory compliance.

16. Exports and permanent store deletion

Before permanently deleting a store, sync pending sales on every device, finish transactions, log staff out and balance and close tills. Receive and save the emailed CSV backup, then confirm it and type the requested deletion text. The deletion removes store-specific operational records; the account, other stores and shared staff remain. Retain records your business needs for tax, refunds, disputes or other legal obligations.

A Pro store allowance is a recurring subscription item. Deleting a store does not itself reduce that purchased allowance: review and reduce it in Plans & billing if no longer required. For an existing separately subscribed Retail shop, the deletion workflow stops its future shop and email-receipt renewals at the end of their current periods. Existing charges and sending usage remain payable. Data deletion does not reverse completed payments or remove provider-held billing records, delivered emails or your own exports.

Orders, dispatch and inventory linking

A manually created order is a draft work instruction. Creating it or sending it to Dispatch Centre does not itself take payment, reserve all stock or record a completed sale. Check products, quantities, prices where applicable and availability, then use the normal sale or packing confirmation workflow.

Forwarded label emails are linked using available source identifiers and order references. An original order date can help review, but a date alone is not proof that two orders are the same. Review ambiguous imports and any label before dispatch. Reimports should not be treated as new sales merely because a label is processed again.

Shared navigation is not an automatic inventory merge. Before configuring stock links or transfers, verify which physical items are already counted and the source and destination. Where an additive merge is offered, matching quantities can be added and double-count the same physical stock; review the explicit warning and confirm only after reconciling the records. Keep appropriate stock and financial records.
