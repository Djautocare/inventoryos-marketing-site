Shipping-label cropping questions

Does it work with every carrier and document layout?

No. Automatic cropping supports particular layouts; changed or unfamiliar files can need manual attention. Always review the complete label and barcode before dispatch.

Can I print labels from my phone?

Use the Mobile print cropped shipping labels option in the Packing Queue, then your device’s supported print or share route. Compatibility depends on your phone, printer and printing app.

Is cropping included in the Free plan?

No. Free lets you begin with stock tools. Label uploads, cropping and Dispatch Centre start with Starter. Workspace email import and QZ Tray direct printing require Pro or Business.

Can I try the email workflow before subscribing to Business?

Create an account, then email hello@inventoryos.co.uk to request one free week of Business. We activate it manually. It does not automatically start a paid subscription. Retail is separate.
