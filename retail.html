GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might be wondering.

Something else on your mind?

Let’s talk
Which plan includes shop and till tools?+

InventoryOS Pro is £19.99/month and includes your first physical store, unlimited tills and staff. Each additional physical store is £10/month. Existing separate shop subscriptions retain their agreed prices.

Will I have an online space to sell from?+

Mi Marché gives you a branded storefront preview linked to eligible InventoryOS stock. Use Seller Centre to prepare your shop, connect branches and add product photos. Storefront previews are available to test now; online checkout is not open yet. Seller payment setup and launch checks must be completed before real payments are enabled. Register your interest on the Mi Marché page for launch updates.

Can I use my existing card reader?+

Yes. Take payment on your existing terminal, then choose Confirm card in InventoryOS. This records your confirmation; InventoryOS does not trigger or independently verify the payment. Card-provider fees are separate.

Do extra tills or staff cost more?+

Pro includes unlimited tills and staff. Your first physical store is included in the £19.99/month Pro subscription. Each additional physical store is £10/month. Two stores cost £29.99/month and three cost £39.99/month, before optional add-ons.

Can I manage a shop and make products in the same plan?+

Yes. Pro includes production, advanced stock tools and your first physical store. Inventory records remain independent until you explicitly configure linking. Existing subscriptions keep their agreed billing unless you choose to change.

What works without an internet connection?+

Prepared back-office cash sales can be saved on the original device and sync when it reconnects. Restricted staff tills need an internet connection. Keep browser data and the original device until pending sales are synced.

How much do customer email receipts cost?+

Printed receipts are included. Email receipts are a separate paid add-on from £2.99/month, with an introductory first month and later usage-based monthly bands. Your Stores & billing page shows the applicable prices and usage before you activate it.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Yes. InventoryOS works in modern mobile and desktop browsers. The Retail dashboard and Till can also be installed as PWAs. Scanner, printer and cash-drawer compatibility depends on your hardware.

Do I need a printer to get started?+

No. The inventory tools work without a printer. Optional direct printing uses QZ Tray, your printer’s normal driver and the InventoryOS printing setup. Mobile printing uses your device’s supported print or share options.

Can I try Pro first?+

Yes. Create an account, then email hello@inventoryos.co.uk to request one free week of Pro. We activate it manually. The app shows the end time and the plan you return to. Complimentary access does not itself create a paid subscription. Existing subscriptions and add-ons continue until separately cancelled.

Is InventoryOS connected to a marketplace?+

InventoryOS is independent software. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vinted, eBay, Meta or Amazon. You remain in control of your marketplace accounts and listings.

Can I keep the Classic layout?+

Yes. Switch the new workspace layout on or off in Settings. An account administrator can lock an account to a chosen layout. The switch changes presentation, not your stock, subscription or permissions.
