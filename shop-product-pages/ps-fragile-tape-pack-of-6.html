Choosing fragile printed packing tape rolls

Fragile printed tape combines parcel sealing with a handling message. The selected option gives the tape width, roll length and quantity supplied.

Check that the roll fits your dispenser. Use appropriate cushioning and outer packaging for the product; a printed warning alone does not protect fragile contents.
