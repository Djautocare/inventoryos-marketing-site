Choosing small bubble wrap rolls for parcels

Small bubble wrap is a flexible option for wrapping suitable products before they go into outer packaging. Roll formats let you cut lengths to suit different items.

Check the roll dimensions and allow space for cushioning inside the parcel. Choose the complete packaging system to suit the goods rather than relying on a single layer.
