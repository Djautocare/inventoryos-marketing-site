Choosing fragile & heavy parcel warning labels

Warning labels help communicate handling information on the outside of a parcel. The available options distinguish fragile contents from heavier packages.

Place the chosen label where it remains readable without covering delivery details or barcodes. Labels do not replace the cushioning and outer packaging your goods need.
