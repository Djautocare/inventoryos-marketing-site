Your InventoryOS workflow

See how the tools fit together, then choose a task to learn more.

Watch the full walkthrough ↗

  1. Add and organise stock

    • Add stock

      Create a product, enter the quantity and choose its storage location. Find it later by name, SKU or barcode.

  2. Record the sale

    • Record an online sale

      For a sale made elsewhere, find the item in Remove Stock and enter its sale price.

    • Sell at the till

      Scan an item, then record cash or confirm payment on your existing card terminal.

    • Set up a Mi Marché shop

      Connect stock, preview the storefront and try a seller test purchase. Public checkout is currently closed.

  3. Pack and dispatch

    • Import, crop and print labels

      Forward supported label emails to your workspace, review the crop and print from the packing queue.

  4. Restock and track costs

    • Reorder and receive stock

      Set a minimum and a target quantity. Mark the order received and put the new units away.

    • Upload receipts and invoices

      Attach a receipt to an expense or review scanned details in the Invoice Inbox before saving.
