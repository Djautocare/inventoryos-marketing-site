A few things to know.

Can I buy or post an item today?

Not yet. Public marketplace links lead here while we build it. At launch, you will need an account before posting; sign-in and account creation are intended to return you to the section you came from.

Do I need to pay for InventoryOS?

No paid InventoryOS plan is needed to register interest. Mi Marché is a separate marketplace project. An optional connection to InventoryOS is planned for commercial sellers.

When will it launch, and what will it cost?

We have not announced a launch date or final marketplace fees. Features, eligible categories and trading terms will be explained before buying and selling open.

Can I suggest something?

Yes. Tell us what matters to you through our contact form or email hello@inventoryos.co.uk. We’re interested in the problems you want a marketplace to solve.
