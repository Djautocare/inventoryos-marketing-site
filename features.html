What InventoryOS does

InventoryOS helps resellers manage stock, sales and dispatch in one web app.

InventoryOS is a browser-based inventory management application for UK resellers. Users can add products, track stock locations, scan barcodes, record sales, manage expenses, upload receipts, prepare shipping labels and view business reports from one account.

On Pro and Business, your workspace has its own shipping-label email address. Forward label attachments to it to import, detect the carrier and crop labels in the Dispatch Centre. You can also upload labels manually.

Email import shows its progress, supports reimporting previous label emails and helps prevent duplicate labels. It does not need access to your Gmail mailbox.

Forward labels from any email provider. Keep your workspace address private and use a label-only forwarding rule.

Manufacturing tools on Business

A clear record of what you use and what you make.

Designed for small-batch makers who need practical stock and production tools alongside their everyday sales workflow.

Materials & packaging

Use the units your work needs.

Track raw materials and packaging separately from whole-item stock. Use each, grams, kilograms, litres, millilitres, metres, centimetres or millimetres, including decimal quantities.

  • Receive batches into named storage areas
  • Record material batch numbers and expiry dates
  • Adjust or relocate remaining quantities with a recorded reason
Product recipes

Define what makes one product.

Create a bill of materials linking a finished product to its components and their quantities per item. Reuse existing products and barcodes or create a new finished item.

  • Convert units within the same measurement type
  • Link equipment usage per batch or per item
  • Edit recipes while keeping completed-run history
Recipe calculator & material orders

Plan several recipes. Order shared ingredients once.

Choose your recipes and finished quantities to see individual requirements and a consolidated ingredient list. Check eligible stock, combine pending material plans and send the shortages to Reordering.

  • Each, grams, litres and metres retain their correct units
  • Shared ingredients are grouped across recipes
  • Supplier links and optional contact details
  • Mark materials ordered and receive partial or complete batches
  • Optional daily recipe email summary: 5pm UK time by default, or your chosen time

Recipe reorder emails require the optional £1.99/month email add-on. Calculations do not reserve materials or complete production.

Production

Make a batch. Update both sides.

Preview the exact material batches, quantities and storage areas needed. When you confirm production is finished, components are deducted and finished stock is added in one operation.

  • Earliest eligible expiry used first
  • Insufficient, held, recalled or expired materials blocked
  • Finished batch, expiry and destination recorded
Batch traceability

Follow a batch through your workflow.

See which material batches made each finished batch, where its remaining stock is located and its recorded movements or dispatch history.

  • Hold or recall batches
  • Source-material holds and recalls block affected finished batches
  • Confirm batch numbers when picking tracked stock
  • Existing opening stock is clearly marked as origin unknown
Equipment & maintenance

Know when to clean or replace.

Set a lifespan such as 30 uses and a cleaning interval such as every 10 operating hours. Log real use manually or record planned recipe usage when completing production.

  • Remaining uses and operating hours
  • Cleaning and replacement due warnings
  • Separate maintenance and lifespan counters
  • History retained when counters restart
Persistent timers

Set the deadline and get on with work.

Start “Clean in 10 hours” or “Products ready in 10 hours” from the app. Deadlines stay saved while you are logged out, with optional emails to your verified account.

  • Minute, hour and day durations
  • Live countdowns and due reminders
  • Acknowledge or cancel timers
  • No automatic stock change or cleaning confirmation

Closing the website does not stop a timer. Reminders resume after a temporary service outage. Elapsed timer time is separate from logged operating hours.

Retail questions

A few things before you open.

Can I use my existing card reader?

Yes. Take the payment on your reader, then choose Confirm card in InventoryOS. The till records your confirmation; it does not verify or trigger the payment.

Do extra tills cost more?

No. Each £19.99/month shop subscription includes unlimited tills and staff. Each additional subscribed shop adds £19.99/month, with its own stock and renewal date.

Can I use a tablet or computer?

Use a supported modern browser and install the Retail dashboard or Till PWA. Scanner, printer and cash-drawer compatibility depends on your hardware; check your setup before trading.

Is Retail included with Business?

Retail is a separate subscription. Your login can open Retail alongside InventoryOS, but Retail shops have separate workspaces, stock and billing from reseller and manufacturing tools.
