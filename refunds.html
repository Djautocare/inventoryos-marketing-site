Refund & Cancellation Policy

This page explains how cancellation, downgrades, failed payments, complimentary access and refund requests work for InventoryOS.

1. Free and paid plans

Free is £0. Starter is £7.99/month and Complete is £19.99/month including one physical store, with additional store allowances at £10/store/month. Existing Pro, Business and separately subscribed Retail customers keep their agreed prices unless they explicitly change. No VAT is currently charged. Optional paid add-ons are confirmed separately.

2. Cancelling

You can cancel or change your paid plan from the billing page inside InventoryOS where the Stripe customer portal is available. Cancellation normally stops future renewals; it does not automatically delete your account or data.

3. After cancellation, downgrade or trial expiry

Paid access normally continues to the confirmed end of the paid period unless the billing confirmation says otherwise. Afterwards, features outside your active plan are restricted. Your account and basic Free access remain available subject to ordinary security controls. Records are not automatically deleted and there is no automatic inventory merge. Usage above the returning plan’s limits may need to be reduced before some actions can continue.

4. Refunds

For business subscriptions, monthly fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started, except where required by law, for a billing error or where we offer a goodwill refund. This does not override the consumer cancellation or other statutory rights described below. If something has gone wrong, contact hello@inventoryos.co.uk with your account email and the issue.

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. If approved, refunds are normally returned through Stripe to the original payment method.

5. Billing errors

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, charged twice, or charged after cancellation, contact us as soon as possible so we can review the Stripe billing records.

6. Beta testers and complimentary access

Some users may receive free access to paid features for internal testing, beta testing or support reasons. Complimentary access is not a paid subscription and can be changed or removed at any time.

7. Plans, store allowances and separate add-ons

Manage the main plan and Complete additional-store allowance through Plans & billing. Reducing the allowance requires a billing change; deleting an individual store does not by itself reduce it. Existing separately subscribed Retail shops and customer email-receipt add-ons are managed in Stores & billing. Existing Pro or Business reminder add-ons have their own billing controls. Cancelling one shop or add-on does not cancel your other subscriptions.

Customer email receipts are £2.99 for the first 30 days, then monthly in arrears at £2.99 for 0–1,000 sends, £4.99 for 1,001–5,000, £9.99 for 5,001–10,000 or £19.99 for 10,001–25,000. Accrued sending usage and other amounts already due remain payable. Review each cancellation confirmation and next-renewal date.

If you cannot open the portal or manage a subscription, email hello@inventoryos.co.uk from your account email, identifying the plan, shop or add-on and your request. Keep the cancellation confirmation.

8. Consumer cancellation and service rights

InventoryOS is designed for business use. If you contract as a consumer and applicable law gives you a cancellation right for an online service, you can normally cancel within 14 days after the contract is made without giving a reason. Email us a clear statement of your decision, identifying your account and subscription. You do not have to use a particular form.

If you expressly request that a service begins during that period, a proportionate charge for service supplied before cancellation may apply only where the required information and consent conditions have been met. Use of the app alone is not treated as an automatic waiver. Any applicable rights where the service is faulty, not as described or not supplied with reasonable care and skill remain unaffected.

Where a statutory cancellation refund is due, we will make it within the applicable legal deadline, normally within 14 days after being told of the cancellation, to the original payment method unless otherwise agreed. Bank or card processing time can affect when it appears. Statutory refunds are not discretionary case-by-case goodwill decisions.

Optional cancellation wording: “I give notice that I cancel my InventoryOS subscription. Account email: [your email]. Subscription or shop: [details]. Contract or order date: [date]. Your name and date of notice: [details].” Send it to hello@inventoryos.co.uk.

9. End of a complimentary trial

A manually granted one-week Complete trial does not itself create a new paid subscription. Existing Pro or Business trials use the plan and expiry shown in the app. At the displayed end time, access returns to the stated prior plan and paid features outside that plan relock. Existing paid subscriptions continue unless separately cancelled. No refund arises from complimentary access itself because no trial fee was paid.

10. Store deletion and refunds to your own customers

Deleting a store is different from cancelling billing. Sync devices and close tills, receive and save the CSV backup, then confirm permanent deletion. Complete additional-store allowances must be reduced separately in Plans & billing. Existing separately subscribed Retail shops and their email-receipt add-ons stop future renewals through the deletion workflow; amounts already due remain payable.

This policy covers purchases from InventoryOS. It does not set the returns policy for goods or services sold by your business to its customers. You remain responsible for those sales, your consumer duties and refunds through your own cash process or card provider.

11. Shop goods: returns and cancellation

Changing your mind: if you buy as a consumer online, tell us you wish to cancel within 14 days after delivery. You then have 14 days after notifying us to return the goods. Email us with your order number and decision to cancel, or send a clear written statement to the address above. You do not have to use a particular form or wait for authorisation to exercise this right.

Send returns to the address above. You pay direct return-postage costs for a change of mind. You may inspect goods as you would in a shop; a deduction may apply only for diminished value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish its nature, characteristics and functioning.

We refund the goods and any standard outbound delivery charge within 14 days after receiving the returned goods or evidence of return, whichever is earlier. The existing printer offer includes free shipping. Any paid delivery charge is shown in the basket and confirmation; refunds of delivery upgrades are limited to the standard outbound delivery amount. Refunds use the original payment method unless otherwise agreed, without a refund fee.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect goods: contact us with the order number and details. Your statutory remedies remain available, including the applicable short-term right to reject faulty goods, repair or replacement rights and further remedies. We cover reasonable return costs where goods are faulty or incorrectly supplied. These rights are separate from change-of-mind cancellation.

Business customers should contact us about returns; consumer cancellation rights apply where the purchase qualifies. Software subscription restrictions do not limit hardware consumer rights.
