Who is Mi Marché for? Second-hand sellers, resellers and small businesses

Selling a few things from home and managing a stockroom are different jobs. We are developing Mi Marché with both in mind: an approachable marketplace for buyers and sellers, with the option to link eligible InventoryOS stock to a storefront.

By the InventoryOS team · 31 October 2026

On this page

  1. For people selling things they no longer use
  2. For independent resellers
  3. For small businesses with physical products
  4. For buyers who enjoy discovering useful things
  5. One marketplace idea, different starting points

For people selling things they no longer use

If you are clearing a shelf, refreshing your wardrobe or moving on from a hobby, you should be able to describe the item clearly without building an entire shop. Our aim is a straightforward listing journey with photographs, condition information and a price.

For second-hand items, show wear and missing parts as carefully as the best features. A buyer needs to understand what is actually included. These are useful habits on any platform while paid Mi Marché checkout is being prepared.

For independent resellers

Resellers often manage many individual products with different conditions and locations. A listing title alone is rarely enough to tell two similar items apart. Consistent references, photos and condition notes help link the item a buyer sees with the one being packed.

Mi Marché is intended to welcome resellers as well as occasional sellers. An optional InventoryOS connection is planned for commercial stock workflows. Read our stockroom-to-marketplace guide for the routine behind that idea.

For small businesses with physical products

An independent business may sell new stock, refurbished goods or products it makes. Each needs a clear description, price and accurate availability. We want Mi Marché to give these businesses another place to be discovered, alongside individual second-hand sellers.

Business and private selling can carry different responsibilities. Seller identification, consumer information and the final marketplace rules will need to be clear before trading opens. We are not announcing that every category or business type will be supported at launch.

For buyers who enjoy discovering useful things

Buying second-hand can mean finding something distinctive or keeping an existing product in use. Buying from an independent business can mean discovering a smaller brand. The goal is to make these products easier to explore through clear listings and sensible categories.

There are no public live listings or purchases yet. Any design preview is a demonstration, not a promise of availability, delivery or buyer protection. Payments, fees and support arrangements will be explained before launch.

One marketplace idea, different starting points

You do not need a paid InventoryOS plan to join the interest list. Pick the option that best describes you, or choose “Just curious”. Registration is not a seller application and does not create a trading account.

Tell us you are interested and we can email you when Mi Marché is ready to use. If your business has a particular need, send us a message. For the wider project goal, start with our introduction to Mi Marché.
