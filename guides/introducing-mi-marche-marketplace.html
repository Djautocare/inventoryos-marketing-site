Introducing Mi Marché: a marketplace for resellers, businesses and second-hand sellers

Good products deserve another opportunity to be found. Mi Marché is a marketplace we are developing to bring everyday second-hand sellers, independent resellers and businesses into one place. Published storefronts can be browsed now. Paid checkout is currently closed.

By the InventoryOS team · 31 October 2026

A place for more than one kind of seller

Some people sell a jacket they no longer wear. Others find, restore or source products as a business. Independent shops may have new stock to offer alongside those pre-loved finds. Our goal for Mi Marché is to make room for those different journeys without expecting everyone to run a large online shop.

Browse published storefronts to see what sellers have listed.

New products and second-hand finds

Mi Marché is designed for more than one product category. Clothing, homeware, electronics and other everyday goods are examples of the kinds of listings we are considering. Used items need clear condition descriptions; new products need accurate pack contents, specifications and prices.

Not every product can be listed. Before trading opens, we will publish the permitted categories, prohibited items and selling rules. Only list items that you are allowed to sell.

Built with the stockroom in mind

InventoryOS already helps people organise stock and locations. Mi Marché Seller Centre lets sellers connect eligible InventoryOS stock and use it in a published storefront.

You will not need an InventoryOS subscription simply to register interest. Browsing is open to visitors. Sign in to InventoryOS to set up a shop.

A clear launch matters more than a hurried one

Listing an item is only part of a sale. Account access, payments, dispatch, support and resolving problems need to fit together. These are part of the work ahead. We have not opened checkout, announced final fees or promised a launch date.

As the project develops, we will explain the buying and selling process and the applicable terms before people are asked to trade. Do not send money or shipping details to register launch interest.

Help shape what comes next

Tell us whether you are interested as a buyer, a private seller, a reseller or a business. That helps us understand who is waiting for the marketplace and what their priorities might be. You can also email hello@inventoryos.co.uk with the friction you would most like a marketplace to remove.

The launch-interest form is separate from our general mailing list. It asks for permission to email you about this launch; it does not create an account or reserve a seller name. Read more about who Mi Marché is for.
